    About the course

    Literacy and Work in the Age of AI asks what it means to read, write, think and build when intelligent tools sit inside everyday work. Rather than studying AI from a distance, the 2027 Young India Fellows worked with it directly, in small interdisciplinary groups.

    Each group identified a real problem around them, on campus, in classrooms, in public health or in how we learn and connect, and designed a working website in response. The projects here are the result: prototypes, tools and reflections, built by humanities and science fellows side by side.
